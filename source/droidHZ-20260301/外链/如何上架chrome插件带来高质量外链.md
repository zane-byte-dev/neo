![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/gG3yexRibCUehfgKdYyBNne1AJjoomWVibapa9ADneyiaOwyDEia8JnsibEZUXAKBwMecAOQle2QcVicJXeWuKao8T3A/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

2.进入后，因为我的网站有订阅，所以我选择了交易者账号

![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/gG3yexRibCUehfgKdYyBNne1AJjoomWViblGibxkJhQraer7gt8lhaXkc7qEnkulSzgm9hKM9rsxm375qyk5rF8ZA/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

3.完善身份，地址（需要地址证明材料），手机号信息里面有些测试账号、服务账号我暂时没有填写，直接保存内容。

![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/gG3yexRibCUehfgKdYyBNne1AJjoomWVibJsDiaPNEUgeCasJia6XJgoiaT4xnkLGcwvAB6HXLFGFmyt7D6TFEQfL1A/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

4.在内容选择上传对应的zip ,把对接插件的文件夹压缩即可

![image](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/gG3yexRibCUehfgKdYyBNne1AJjoomWVibZK7GrOQOw32pRVJD4Eh875LDe59xEGvr9m6rictKeole0ao9jYnPVaw/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)
